## Resolution

Study 072 is a civic-instrument dashboard: an isometric SVG model town (matrix projection `P=(x-y)·.866U,(x+y)·.5U-z·U`, per-face `shade()` ramps, depth-sorted) on a plinth with painted lore ("EST. 1926", district names), caption tiles, a ticker tape, and a switchboard with corner screws — light palette `#F6F1EA/#FDFBF7/#2B2D42` with `#FFD166` as the single energy accent, and one `mix-blend-mode:multiply` rect tinting the whole board by time of day. Its analogies map 1:1 onto the Platform voice seed (buildings=miniapps, wires=traffic with speed ∝ load, substation=platform, feeders=flipable capabilities, interconnector=legacy deps). Top borrows: the SVG isometric construction + plinth inscription chrome; the hard-offset-shadow/kicker/meter-card chrome kit with the exact palette; throughput-as-motion dash-flow with its reduced-motion static-arrowhead substitute. Leave the full simulation physics, flavor sprawl, and unwired gauges. Full distillation: `../findings/r3-platform-town-grid.md`.
